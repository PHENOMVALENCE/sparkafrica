Our Purpose

Mission and Vision driving our impact

Our Mission

To accelerate inclusive, sustainable growth by expanding digital skills, infrastructure, and entrepreneurship ensuring every African can participate meaningfully in the digital economy.

Our Vision

A continent where tech-driven innovation powers equitable prosperity and every community can shape its own digital destiny.

Our Journey

From Sparked Africa to Digital Economy Africa

Founded in 2023 as Sparked Africa, we initially focused on digital inclusion for youth and women. As our impact grew, so did our ambition.

In 2025, we evolved into Digital Economy Africa (DEA)—a pan-African catalyst for digital transformation, policy advocacy, and tech entrepreneurship.

While our name changed, our commitment to people-first technology remains at the core.

How We Drive Impact

Three interconnected pillars moving ideas from concept to scaled impact

Social Innovation & Impact

We co-design inclusive, tech-powered solutions with the communities we serve, prioritising youth, women, and other marginalised voices so that no one is left behind.

Data & Evidence for Development

Our data scientists and researchers turn raw information into actionable insights that shape policy, optimise service delivery, and illuminate progress toward the SDGs.

Partnerships & Capacity Building

We convene governments, academia, private-sector innovators, and grassroots organisations, while strengthening local leadership through up-skilling and mentorship programmes.
